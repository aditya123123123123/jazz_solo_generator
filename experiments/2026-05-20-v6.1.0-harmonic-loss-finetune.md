# v6.1.0 Literal Harmonic-Loss Finetune on M4 Mac Mini

Date: 2026-05-20

## Headline

Restoring the dropped v5b harmonic auxiliary loss recovered strong chord sensitivity in v6.1.0. Phrase conditioning also matters again. Artist conditioning remains weak.

## Motivation

The 2026-05-18 conditioning ablation showed that v6 had effectively collapsed its conditioning pathways: chord, phrase, and artist changes barely affected pitch CE, and zeroing chord/phrase inputs even improved loss. Archaeology pointed to a likely cause: v6 dropped the v5b harmonic auxiliary loss during the train_v6 rewrite.

This run tests whether a literal port of the v5b harmonic loss into v6 recovers conditioning behavior.

## Conditioning Ablation

100,000 validation windows, batch size 512, seed 42.

| condition | v6.1 pitch CE | v6 pitch CE | delta v6.1 - v6 | delta vs v6.1 baseline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| baseline | 2.0211 | 5.0001 | -2.9790 | -- |
| phrase_zeroed | 2.1958 | 4.9680 | -2.7723 | +0.1747 |
| chord_zeroed | 2.1855 | 4.9388 | -2.7533 | +0.1644 |
| artist_zeroed | 2.0420 | 5.0002 | -2.9583 | +0.0208 |
| chord_shuffled | 2.1896 | 5.0005 | -2.8109 | +0.1685 |
| phrase_shuffled | 2.1480 | 4.9994 | -2.8514 | +0.1269 |

Success classification: Strong.

The pre-committed strong-success threshold was `delta chord_shuffled >= +0.10` pitch CE. v6.1.0 reached `+0.1685`.

## Interpretation

- Chord sensitivity recovered strongly: wrong or missing chord conditioning now costs roughly `+0.16` to `+0.17` pitch CE.
- Phrase conditioning also recovered: zeroing or shuffling phrase IDs now costs roughly `+0.13` to `+0.17` pitch CE.
- Artist conditioning remains weak: zeroing artist ID costs only `+0.0208` pitch CE.

The headline conclusion is that restoring the dropped v5b harmonic auxiliary loss recovered the conditioning behavior that v6 had lost. v6.1.0 is therefore the new best checkpoint for conditioned generation experiments.
