Architecture as a musical hypothesis.

The generator plans phrase-level intent first, then realizes notes against chord changes, style conditioning, note history, and inference-time musical guardrails.

model pipeline

PhrasePlanner

Maps chord progressions + artist conditioning into phrase-cluster tokens.

chordsartist idphrase clusters

NoteExecutor

Renders phrase intent into pitch, duration, and rest events.

pitchdurationrest

Audition layer

Outputs are judged through MIDI/WAV/MP3 bundles, rhythm-section verification, harmonic metrics, phrase faithfulness, and listening decisions.

Why this site exists

The progress is not a simple leaderboard. Several “better” checkpoints became less musical. This preserves the real arc: conditioning collapse, harmonic repair, phrase shaping, mechanical correctness, vocabulary, and listening.

The evolution map.

Each era changed what the project meant by “better.”

bootstrap

Corpus → phrase tokens

WJazzD phrases, features, tokenizers, and 64 phrase clusters become the symbolic vocabulary.

foundation
v5/v6

Hierarchical generator

PhrasePlanner + NoteExecutor become an end-to-end solo pipeline with musical durations, augmentation, tempo conditioning, and a fast v6 cache.

architecture
v6.1

Conditioning collapse diagnosed

Ablations show v6 was playing notes but not strongly listening to chord/phrase conditioning; harmonic auxiliary loss is restored.

repair
v6.4

Phrase-aware generation

Whole-progression phrase planning and phrase-faithfulness metrics reconnect generation to long-form musical intent.

phrasing
v6.5

Scientific musicality loop

One-variable experiments fix register, repetition, cadence, and contour. Exp 10 adds controlled bebop vocabulary and becomes the first clear musical win.

breakthrough
v6.6

Safety-vs-color trap

Expanded vocabulary fine-tuning becomes safer but blander. It is explicitly not promoted.

not promoted
