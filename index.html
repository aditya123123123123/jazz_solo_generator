Why this site exists

The progress is not a simple leaderboard. Several “better” checkpoints became less musical. This preserves the real arc: conditioning collapse, harmonic repair, phrase shaping, mechanical correctness, vocabulary, and listening.

How this site will stay current

New JazzMLProject updates should add three things together: the version/change summary, the audition audio, and the listening verdict. That keeps the public story honest instead of becoming a stale leaderboard.

1. New changeArchitecture, training data, checkpoint, or inference-control change.
2. New outputRepresentative audio for the same comparison tunes when possible, plus broader audition bundles.
3. New verdictAccepted, rejected, baseline, or under-audition — preferably after listening, not just metrics.

audio rendering upgrade

The listening renders now sound more like a jazz combo.

The MIDI-backed v6.8/v6.8.1 WAV auditions were re-rendered with a tenor sax lead, comping piano, walking bass, jazz drum kit, light timing/velocity humanization, room ambience, compression, and loudness normalization. The underlying generated notes are unchanged — this is a production/rendering upgrade, not a new model version.

Legacy v6.5/v6.6 MP3-only bundles were also remastered for cleaner playback. Their source MIDI was not present in the published artifacts, so those older files are improved masters rather than full re-instrumentations.
